About this project

A record of what reading makes us think.

Between the Lines is a personal reading-footprint project documenting a year of reading across different subjects. It brings together reflection, interpretation and evaluation rather than treating books as isolated assignments.

The aim is simple: to look beyond what a book says and consider how it says it, why it matters, and who might benefit from reading it.

“The most interesting part of a book may be the question it leaves unanswered.”

— A guiding idea behind this reading footprint
