Two main things I learnt from these books

01 · PERSPECTIVE

Perspectives and how they shape messages

Perspective, in my opinion, is very influential towards the message an author tries to send, especially when it comes to political and/or historical texts. It's important to filter carefully and determine which statements may contain bias.

02 · FICTION

How fiction can still teach

Fiction books, despite mainly being false events, can still teach important concepts to the audience. The author is able to do this by telling stories through a fictional character and allowing the reader to process and try to find a real world connection.
